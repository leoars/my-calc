# Third-party software, artwork and trademarks

My Calc is an independent project, distributed under the Apache License 2.0 (see `LICENSE`).

## Third-party software

My Calc has no third-party dependencies. It is written in Swift and uses only the frameworks that ship with macOS (AppKit and its companions). The arithmetic is its own: a decimal number type and the functions of the scientific mode, in `Sources/BigDecimal.swift` and `Sources/BigMath.swift`.

## Artwork and text

All icons, images and interface texts in My Calc are original: they were created for this project (the images are drawn in code) and are covered by the Apache License 2.0. The application icon (a glossy blue pocket calculator in three-quarter view, with “My” on a cream display, cream keys, a light-blue column of operators and an orange equals key, in the style of the other icons of the My suite) is generated by `Tools/icon.swift` into `Resources/MyCalc.icns`. The keys, the display and the other parts of the window are drawn by the application itself.

No artwork, icon or text was copied from Microsoft Calculator or Microsoft Windows.

## Fonts

My Calc does not include any font files. The interface uses the system font of macOS.
